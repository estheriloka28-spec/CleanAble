📚 Environmental Knowledge Hub

Learn proper waste sorting practices and help reduce landfill overflow across Nigeria.

💡 Did you know? Proper waste sorting can reduce landfill waste by up to 70% and creates jobs in Nigeria's growing recycling sector.

🧴 Plastic Waste Recyclable

🍶

Water Bottles (PET)

Rinse before disposal. Accepted by most recyclers. High resale value.

🛍️

Nylon / Polythene Bags

Bundle together before disposal. Many supermarkets have drop-off points.

📦

Packaging Materials

Flatten cardboard. Remove food residue from containers before sorting.

🪣

Plastic Buckets

Clean and separate from general waste. HDPE plastics are highly recyclable.

🍃 Organic Waste Compostable

🍎

Food Scraps

Fruit peels, vegetable cuttings, and leftovers can be composted for garden use.

🌿

Plant & Garden Waste

Leaves, grass clippings, and plant trimmings are excellent compost material.

☕

Coffee & Tea Waste

Coffee grounds and tea bags are nitrogen-rich and excellent for composting.

🥚

Eggshells

Eggshells add calcium to compost. Bones should be disposed of separately.

🗑️ General Waste Non-recyclable

🩹

Sanitary Items

Wrap securely before disposal. These cannot be recycled or composted.

🪟

Broken Glass & Ceramics

Wrap in newspaper before disposing. Do not mix with recyclable glass.

🧻

Soiled Paper Products

Used tissue and greasy takeaway bags go into general waste only.

🔋

Electronics & Batteries

Take to designated e-waste collection points — contain toxic materials.

🌍

The 3R Principle

Before disposing of any item, ask yourself: Can I Reduce my use of it? Can I Reuse it? Can I Recycle it?
